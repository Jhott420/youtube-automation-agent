# TikTok Shop Starter Kit: 3 Products

Listings and video scripts for the three starter products. Copy them straight into TikTok Seller Center.

**Before you paste anything:** replace every `[BRACKETED]` value with the real number from your supplier's product page in CJdropshipping. TikTok removes listings whose claims don't match the product, and a wrong size or weight claim turns into a "not as described" return.

## Listing rules this copy follows

These are general TikTok Shop listing practices. Check Seller Center's current policy pages too, because the rules change.

- Titles state what the product is, with no "best", "#1", "cheapest" or "free shipping"
- No medical, health or guaranteed-result claims
- No ALL-CAPS words and no emoji in titles
- No other brands' names
- No claims about speed, capacity or materials that the supplier hasn't confirmed

## Posting plan for the first 10 days

| Day | What to post |
|---|---|
| 1–3 | 1 video per product per day, using Scripts 1–3 |
| 4–7 | 2 per day on whichever product had the most views and add-to-carts; 1 per day on the others |
| 8–10 | Go all in on the winner with Scripts 4–5 plus remakes of your best video. Turn on Open Collaboration at 15–20% commission for it. |

**Video tips that apply to all three:**
- Put the hook as text on screen in the first second. Most viewers watch muted.
- Show the result within the first 3 seconds.
- Keep videos 15–25 seconds long.
- Pin the product in every video with TikTok Shop's product link.
- Reply to comments with a video. These replies often get more reach than the original post.
